Professional Profile
The dentist.

Dr. Mohamed Abdelbadie holds a BDS in Oral and Dental Medicine and Surgery from Sinai University (2021). He began his career at Dayrut Central Hospital and Ghamra Military Hospital, then practised at the Khder, Enaya and Ibtisama dental centres, before founding Smile Center in Dayrut in 2025, where he serves as Clinical Director. He is classified by the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS) with a positive DataFlow verification. Alongside his clinical practice, he builds complete software systems for the medical and commercial sectors — most notably the Bahbah Pharmacy platform and the Al-Sawaf fabric-store system.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

With modern anaesthesia techniques and microscopic treatment, root canal sessions are performed with virtually no pain in the vast majority of cases — and the original inflammation pain ends as soon as treatment begins.

How long does a root canal session take?

Usually between 45 and 90 minutes depending on the number of canals and the complexity of the case; many cases are completed in a single visit.

Do I need a crown after root canal treatment?

For most back teeth, a crown is recommended after root canal treatment to protect the tooth from fracture and restore full chewing function.
